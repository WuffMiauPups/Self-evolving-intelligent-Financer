# SETAI Research Adoption Roadmap

**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Scope:** Synthesis of 24 paper digests + two codebase gap analyses (memory/continual-learning/self-evolution lens; trading-strategy-evaluation lens) into a single prioritized adoption plan.
**Judged against the project vision:** (1) the bot develops strategies on its own, (2) it also uses real-world known strategies under soft caps/limits, and (3) it changes its weights based on realized trading outcomes.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

SETAI has the *skeleton* of a self-evolving trading system but not the *loop*. Across 24 papers spanning continual learning, memory-augmented models, self-improvement, AutoML/meta-learning, and market simulation, one conclusion dominates: **the highest-leverage work is not adding new learning machinery — it is closing and hardening the feedback loop that already exists in stub form, and making evaluation honest enough that the loop cannot lie to itself.**

Today, the retrain trigger (`src/training/retrain.py`), replay buffer, RL scaffold, and self-supervised modules are RESERVED and unwired. Checkpoint promotion is decorative — `run_train.py` overwrites `brain.pt` ungated and the runtime never reads the registry. The associative memory bank computes outcome statistics that feed back into **nothing** (`memory_policy.py` explicitly documents that memory does not gate, veto, or adjust trades). Meanwhile, the evaluation stack grades every candidate with same-bar-close fills at flat 2 bps, zero training transaction costs, walk-forward folds that never retrain (so "out-of-sample" numbers are not out-of-sample), untradeable baselines, and a split-unadjusted historical cache. Any self-evolution built on top of this measures noise.

The literature converges on a specific ordering. **Phase 1** (highest impact, lowest risk): fix evaluation honesty (next-bar fills, spread-dependent slippage, real cost assumptions, CVaR, tradeable benchmarks, per-fold retraining, paired significance tests), wire the retrain loop and make promotion authoritative, and make retraining forgetting-safe with the cheapest proven anchors — knowledge distillation to the previous promoted checkpoint, an L2 proximal term, regime-stratified rehearsal, and a backward-transfer promotion gate (NFL+, DEN, TACO, Self-Net all independently prescribe this). Also fix credit assignment per the constrained-RL shield principle: record the *executed* (risk-clamped) action, never raw intent. **Phase 2**: make memory causal — blend retrieved outcome priors into trade proposals, then credit each memory only for the PnL delta it counterfactually caused (D2Skill), with novelty-gated writes, protected rare-regime episodes, and utility-based eviction replacing FIFO. Replace the `force_directional_flip` symptom-patch with cost-aware label reshaping (JaxMARL-HFT's degeneracy diagnosis). **Phase 3**: build the "known strategies with soft caps" arbitration layer, familiarity/OOD gating, regime snapshots, cross-sectional features, and smarter sweeps (BOHB, Kendall-Tau proxy audits). **Phase 4** (experiment-gated): population-based training, SOM regime maps, differentiable Hopfield retrieval, meta-gradients, capacity growth.

Equally important is the explicit **do-not-do list** (Section 5): no GAN market generators yet, no per-bar test-time RL, no wholesale restoration of the deleted ESOM/Hopfield stack, no full NAS, no pattern-completion forecasting, and no quantum/NLS pricing models. Every self-modification mechanism must beat a plain fine-tune baseline and an equal-budget random-search control before adoption.

## 2. Paper Digests (24)

### 2.1 LADDER: Self-Improving LLMs Through Recursive Problem Decomposition
**Technique:** A self-improvement loop where a model facing problems too hard for RL recursively generates trees of easier variants, then trains with GRPO using only a cheap automated verifier as reward. RL directly on hard problems collapses (0% within 30 steps); RL on the self-generated easy-to-hard curriculum reaches 82%. TTRL extends this to test time: generate variants of an unsolved problem, run ~100 RL steps, answer, roll back weights.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** SETAI's realized-PnL updates are exactly the "RL without variants" failure mode — noisy, sparse intraday reward invites the collapse LADDER documents. The transferable recipe: (a) build a difficulty gradient over market data (rank training windows by trend/vol clarity, class balance, memory-retrieval confidence and schedule the retrain loop easy→hard); (b) SETAI already has LADDER's precondition, a cheap verifier (backtest engine + forward-path targets); (c) the frozen-broad-brain + regime-conditional-adaptation split matches "TTRL only works after LADDER"; (d) their anti-gaming verifier filters are analogous to SETAI's degeneracy fixes — any outcome loop needs explicit degenerate-policy rejection (always-flat, always-long).
**Top idea:** Adaptive difficulty scheduling over the replay buffer — advance to harder regime buckets only when the current bucket's directional accuracy clears a threshold; demote on degradation **[low difficulty]**. Also: curriculum-ordered retraining [medium], verifier-hardening audit of the reward path [low], synthetic easier-variant regimes for cold start [medium], TTRL-style episodic head fine-tune before low-confidence decisions [high].
**Key caveat:** Markets lack a perfect verifier — backtest replay verifies label consistency, not decision quality; synthetic variants risk training on fictional distributions; and any outcome-driven update still needs a KL/trust-region anchor to the canonical brain, curriculum or not.

### 2.2 Temporal Self-Rewarding Language Models
**Technique:** Diagnoses contrast collapse in iterative self-improvement: as a model improves, its self-generated "chosen" and "rejected" samples converge (9x score-gap shrinkage), provably vanishing the DPO gradient. Fix: anchor rejected samples to the frozen initial model (past) and upgrade chosen samples via a temporary next-generation model (future), sustaining a wide preference gap at the same compute.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** SETAI's closed loop (retrain on own outcomes → promote → generate new trades → retrain) is structurally the same and exposed to the same degeneracy: as the promoted brain narrows its behavior, positive/negative training examples converge in the state-encoder embedding space and update signal shrinks — precisely the degeneracy class recent commits fought. Anchored rejection maps to keeping the frozen canonical brain/BaselineMLP as a permanent source of counterfactual "bad" decisions and retaining old losing episodes; future-guided chosen maps to SETAI's isolated experiment checkpoints (adopt a candidate's decisions as targets only where they demonstrably out-score the incumbent). Best-checkpoint-is-often-not-last validates promotion-by-evaluation.
**Top idea:** A contrast-collapse monitor in the retrain loop — log the outcome-score gap and embedding cosine similarity between positive/negative training examples per iteration; block promotion when the gap collapses **[low]**. Also: anchored negatives from the frozen brain [medium], future-guided positive selection via experiment checkpoints [high], economic-margin pair-validity filter (gap > round-trip cost + noise buffer) [low], best-iteration promotion [low], stronger judge (regime-conditioned, risk-adjusted, calibrated outcome scores) [medium].
**Key caveat:** The vanishing-gradient theorem is DPO-specific; only the qualitative failure mode transfers. Anchoring negatives to a stale-regime M0 conflicts with non-stationarity — anchors need regime conditioning or periodic re-baselining.

### 2.3 MemMamba: Rethinking Memory Patterns in State Space Models
**Technique:** Formalizes exponential forgetting in SSMs and fixes it with a "Note Block": salience-thresholded writes of compressed token summaries into a tiny fixed pool (50 slots × 64 dims), uncertainty-triggered cross-attention reads fused back into the state, priority (not FIFO) eviction — all at O(n). Also introduces cheap linear-probe fidelity metrics (ETMF/ECLMF) that rank architectures on memory retention.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** A near-blueprint for how the memory bank should interact with the brain: gate *both directions* — write only salient episodes (feature surprise, regime flip, large realized outcome), read only when the brain is uncertain (policy entropy, low calibrated confidence). This keeps the bank small and high-signal, which is prerequisite for clean outcome credit assignment. The session-scoped note pool pattern gives the fixed-receptive-field CNN encoder effectively unbounded intraday lookback (condition on the open at 2pm) at O(1) cost. The ridge-probe is a ready-made encoder-degeneracy detector.
**Top idea:** Dual-threshold gating in `updater.py` (write-gate salience score) and `memory_policy.py` (read-gate uncertainty score) **[medium]**. Also: session note pool with cross-attention [medium], ECLMF-style fidelity probe per checkpoint [low], priority eviction by salience × outcome-informativeness × retrieval usage [low], differentiable retrieval [high], MemMamba-style encoder only if multi-day minute lookbacks are ever adopted [high].
**Key caveat:** All evidence is language/synthetic retrieval where "importance" is exactly recoverable; market salience is only knowable in hindsight and learned scorers can overfit noise. The raw long-context capability is irrelevant at SETAI's lookbacks — adopt the design patterns, not the architecture. Under-review paper, single seed, some adjusted tables.

### 2.4 Self-Improving AI → AI & Human Co-Improvement (Meta FAIR position paper)
**Technique:** Position paper: prefer human-in-the-loop "co-improvement" over autonomous self-improvement. Main artifact: a five-axis taxonomy of self-improvement (parameters, training data, objective, architecture, code) with per-axis failure modes (reward hacking, drift from priors, unvalidated self-judgments).
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The taxonomy maps directly onto governance of SETAI's self-evolution: today it self-improves only on the parameters axis (and partially data). The user's vision is exactly the paper's prescription — autonomy on low axes, human-gated control on high axes (objective changes, architecture growth, strategy code behind the promotion gate). "Reward hacking without strong priors" is the trading analog of fitting regime luck; the remedy (verifiable fixed evaluation targets + strong priors) argues for a frozen known-strategies prior and a versioned regime-stratified walk-forward benchmark that every self-update must pass.
**Top idea:** Formalize a "self-evolution ladder" in configs — per-axis autonomy policy, with parameters/data autonomous and objective/architecture/code human-gated **[low]**. Also: fixed versioned regime-stratified benchmark suite as an RLVR-analog promotion gate [medium], KL/L2 drift guard toward a human-approved prior [medium], self-challenging synthetic scenarios mined from the bot's own failures [high], automated error-analysis artifact per retrain surfaced in the GUI [low].
**Key caveat:** Zero experiments — a normative institutional stance. All ideas are analogical translations, not validated methods; LLM self-play dynamics don't straightforwardly transfer to low-SNR intraday data.

### 2.5 D2Skill: Dynamic Dual-Granularity Skill Bank for Agentic RL
**Technique:** Couples RL with a self-maintained skill bank at two granularities (task-level and step-level). Core mechanism: paired baseline (no skill) vs skill-injected rollouts under the same policy give *counterfactual* per-skill utility (Δ vs baseline), tracked by EMA; retrieval ranks by similarity + utility + UCB exploration bonus; capacity-bounded pruning by the same score with a protection window for young skills. Skills get internalized into policy weights over time, after which their marginal utility decays and the pruner retires them.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** This is the maintenance half of SETAI's memory system, in principled form. Three missing mechanisms: (1) counterfactual credit — replay the same bars brain-only vs memory-fused and credit each retrieved memory with the PnL delta *only when fusion changed the action* (vs today's correlational attribution); (2) utility+UCB retrieval and capacity-bounded pruning (the paper's biggest ablation win, 57.8→72.7); (3) dual granularity = regime-keyed strategy-level guidance (strategy family, exposure soft caps — the user's vision item 2) plus per-bar episodic corrections. The internalization lifecycle (memory lesson → brain absorbs it → utility decays → pruned) is a concrete model of self-evolving weights without unbounded memory growth.
**Top idea:** Counterfactual memory-utility scoring via paired brain-only/memory-fused backtest replays, utility EMA per item **[medium]**. Also: UCB bonus in retrieval ranking [low], capacity-bounded pruning with protection window [low], dual-granularity memory tiers [medium], reflection-on-failure trade autopsy [high], paired baseline/fused walk-forward as the memory-bank promotion gate [low].
**Key caveat:** Validated on episodic text environments with clean binary success; the hindsight gap is far higher-variance on market PnL and needs many paired samples or per-regime normalization. Utility can flip sign on regime change — track it regime-conditioned.

### 2.6 AiGAS-dEVL-RC: Adaptive Growing Neural Gas for Recurrently Drifting Streams
**Technique:** Unlabeled streaming classifier that stores compact distribution-level snapshots (GNG nodes + prototypes) of past concepts, matches incoming batches against memory with a two-stage test (cheap centroid prefilter, then shape-overlap), and *snaps back* instantly to a stored concept when a regime recurs — no labels, no retraining. Novelty-gated storage keeps memory compact; a projection step keeps stored prototypes retrievable under slow drift.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The blueprint for the missing regime-recurrence layer: a library of past market regimes re-activated wholesale (per-regime Platt params, retrieval weights, sizing soft caps) instead of relearned by the retrain loop. Its novelty-gated write rule directly fixes SETAI's near-duplicate memory pollution (retrieval currently dominated by adjacent bars of the same symbol, including the query bar at sim 1.0). Prequential error over resolved trades is the correct standing live-monitoring metric for rollback/retrain triggers.
**Top idea:** Novelty-gate memory writes in `updater.py` — merge near-duplicate keys into existing entries (update stats/count) instead of inserting **[low]**. Also: regime-snapshot memory with two-stage matching using MMD/Mahalanobis (not alpha-shapes) [medium], prototype drift projection for stored keys [medium], two-stage retrieval prefilter by regime partition [low], unsupervised abrupt-drift detector tightening risk caps [medium], prequential-error live monitoring [low].
**Key caveat:** Evidence is on synthetic 2–5D streams with exactly repeating distributions; market regimes recur approximately, matching must be soft, alpha-shape IoU is infeasible beyond ~5–6 dims, and all thresholds were hand-tuned per dataset. Use retrieved regime knowledge as a prior/gate, never a hard model swap.

### 2.7 TACO + RePro: Topology-Aware Graph Coarsening for Continual Graph Learning
**Technique:** Rehearsal-based continual learning storing all past knowledge as one bounded coarsened graph: train on new + compressed-old data (implicit replay), then re-coarsen to budget by merging embedding-similar node pairs (RePro, reuses already-computed encoder embeddings, O(edges)). Node Fidelity Preservation protects a buffer of minority-class nodes from merging — Theorem 4.1 proves naive consolidation systematically erases minority classes. Evaluation via the AP/AF (average performance / average forgetting) matrix.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Regimes recur rather than being disjoint — exactly the generalized class-IL setting where compressed rehearsal beats weight-penalty regularization. The coarsened graph is a bounded memory where similar episodes merge into importance-weighted prototypes rather than being evicted; Node Fidelity Preservation is a formal treatment of SETAI's exposure: under capacity-bounded consolidation or class rebalancing, rare crisis episodes are statistically the first to vanish — the exact memories the VETO/risk path needs most. AP/AF is a ready-made upgrade to walk-forward promotion: promote on average performance AND bounded forgetting.
**Top idea:** Protected set in the memory bank (reservoir sample + tagged tail episodes exempt from pruning/merge via merge-score penalty) **[low]**. Also: RePro-style consolidation (merge to weighted prototypes instead of evict) [medium], rehearsal-based retraining with per-regime quotas [medium], AP/AF matrix in walk-forward + promotion gate [low], coarsened regime-transition graph as expected-path prior [high], minority-quota protection in class_balance/calibration data [low].
**Key caveat:** Markets have no natural graph — topology must be synthetic (temporal/k-NN edges), possibly weakening the headline claim. Task boundaries are fuzzy, labels delayed/noisy; joint training remained the upper bound.

### 2.8 Safe and Compliant Cross-Market Trade Execution (Constrained RL + Shield)
**Technique:** Execution as a Constrained MDP: PPO policy + runtime Shield that projects unsafe actions onto the nearest feasible action, treated as part of environment dynamics (the agent learns from *executed* outcomes), plus training-time penalties on would-be violations. Findings: cost of compliance ≈ zero; constraints act as variance/tail-risk reducers; a "shield-off" toggle shows policies never internalize constraints from penalties alone; cap sweeps reveal a sharp knee. Paired-day evaluation with t-tests and CVaR95.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Formalizes SETAI's existing pattern (learned policy → deterministic risk override → execution) and exposes a live credit-assignment bug: memory episodes and training targets must record the post-risk-manager *executed* action plus an intervention flag — otherwise self-evolving updates attribute PnL to actions never taken (today `record_snapshot` passes `action_annotation=None`). The shield-divergence auxiliary loss teaches the brain to live inside its caps (fewer VETO conflicts); cap sweeps and paired-seed statistics make promotion decisions defensible at intraday-sized edges. Caps-as-variance-reducers reframes the risk layer as improving learning-signal quality, not taxing the brain.
**Top idea:** Record executed (clamped/vetoed) actions + intervention flags in memory and training targets; expose intervention rate in runtime_status **[low]**. Also: shield-divergence auxiliary loss [medium], cap-sweep harness (mean/var/CVaR vs cap, pick the knee) [low], guardrail-toggle diagnostic across retrain generations [low], paired-seed evaluation + CVaR95 in promotion [low], Planner/policy budget split [medium].
**Key caveat:** Solves execution of a known parent order in a simulator with a mean-reverting fundamental — says nothing about alpha. The zk audit layer is mocked and irrelevant to a single-user bot. Transfer the methodology, not the constraints.

### 2.9 DPSOM / T-DPSOM: Deep Probabilistic Clustering with Self-Organizing Maps
**Technique:** Trains a probabilistic SOM jointly with a VAE: soft Student-t cluster assignments (DEC-style CAH loss) plus a Soft-SOM loss that spills assignment probability onto grid neighbors, yielding interpretable 2D topology essentially for free. T-DPSOM adds latent smoothness across timesteps and an LSTM forecasting p(z_{t+1}|z_t); ICU experiments show unsupervised cells carry information about future severity.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The principled version of the deleted `esom_map.py`/`regime_map.py`: a discrete, topology-preserving regime grid over the state encoder's latent stream, with per-cell realized-outcome statistics (win rate, expectancy, MAE/MFE) as a regime-conditioned prior for fusion. SOM cells are natural coarse memory keys (retrieve cell + toroidal neighbors, giving legitimate neighbor smoothing for sparse cells — cold-start mitigation). Assignment entropy is a free regime-ambiguity scalar for position sizing and the VETO gate; the latent LSTM gives a regime-transition early-warning.
**Top idea:** Soft-assignment entropy as a regime-ambiguity gate scaling position size / raising the VETO bar **[low]** — but only after a PSOM head exists, so effectively: PSOM auxiliary head behind experiment isolation **[medium]**. Also: cell-indexed memory bank with neighbor smoothing [medium], latent smoothness loss [low], latent LSTM transition forecast [high], Moran's-index acceptance test + loss-magnitude hyperparameter heuristics [low], GUI regime-map tab [medium].
**Key caveat:** Even in the favorable medical domain, enrichment NMIs are tiny (~0.08–0.11); per-cell expectancy is a prior/gate, never alpha. Fully offline — grids go stale and cell IDs are unstable across refits. Re-adds multi-loss balancing complexity SETAI just deleted; gate behind experiment checkpoints (P3).

### 2.10 DEN: Lifelong Learning with Dynamically Expandable Networks
**Technique:** Grows a sparse network as tasks arrive: selective retraining of only the affected subnetwork (sparse probe + BFS through nonzero weights), threshold-triggered expansion with group-lasso pruning (task difficulty sizes the growth), and split/duplication of semantically drifted units with timestamped inference (task t uses only units existing at stage t).
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Treat regimes as tasks: selective retraining stops a calm-bull retrain from overwriting bear/high-vol weights; threshold-triggered expansion is a principled trigger for "the bot grows strategy capacity when markets present genuinely novel structure" (vision item 1); the per-unit drift metric ρ_i = ||w_t − w_{t−1}|| is a cheap quantitative degeneracy diagnostic; unit/prototype splitting maps to splitting memory prototypes whose outcome stats bifurcate across regimes.
**Top idea:** L2-anchor (or Fisher-weighted) penalty λ||W − W_promoted||² in the retrain loss, exposed in `configs/training.yaml` **[low]**. Also: per-unit/per-module drift logging with a promotion gate [low], regime-conditioned selective retraining via sparse masks [medium], memory-prototype splitting on bimodal outcome stats [medium], threshold-triggered head expansion with group lasso [high], DEN-Finetune as an offline capacity estimator [medium].
**Key caveat:** Assumes known task identity at inference; SETAI's regime classifier is noisy, and misdetection routes to the wrong subnetwork. SETAI keeps full history — simple rehearsal may capture most anti-forgetting benefit at a fraction of the cost and must be the baseline DEN-style mechanisms beat. Five interacting hyperparameters; nontrivial PyTorch implementation.

### 2.11 Self-Replication in Neural Networks (ALIFE)
**Technique:** Studies networks operating on their own weights: repeated self-application collapses random nets to the trivial all-zero fixpoint; hand-built non-zero fixpoints die under 1e-9 noise; *self-training* (training a net to reproduce its own representation) reliably creates stable non-trivial fixpoints; a population "soup" with attack/learn interactions emergently clusters at non-trivial self-replicators.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The dynamical lesson: any system repeatedly updating its own weights from self-generated signals has strong attractors toward degenerate fixpoints — the weight-space analog of the always-flat collapse SETAI already patched behaviorally. Transferable mechanisms: self-training = self-distillation as an anti-forgetting/stability anchor between promotions; the soup + fitness = population-based training with cross-distillation (a better substrate for "develops strategies on its own" than a single collapsible canonical brain); ε-fixpoints give a formal stagnation detector; the noise-brittleness result motivates a noise-robustness promotion gate (sharp-minimum checkpoints fail).
**Top idea:** Weight-space trajectory telemetry — per-layer norms + PCA projection per retrain, alerting on monotonic shrink toward zero or frozen checkpoints **[low]**. Also: self-distillation anchor across checkpoint generations [medium], noise-robustness promotion gate (perturb weights, require stable Sharpe/action entropy/VETO rates) [low], ε-fixpoint stagnation detector with perturb-then-distill exploration [medium], fitness-augmented population of 4–8 brains [high].
**Key caveat:** Tiny linear networks, no task, no market content — value is diagnostic and architectural by analogy. Consult the PBT literature before implementing the population idea. A stable fixpoint is not a goal in markets; use as diagnostics/stabilizers only.

### 2.12 AutoML: A Survey of the State-of-the-Art (He et al. 2021)
**Technique:** Full-pipeline AutoML survey: data prep, feature engineering search, NAS (search spaces × optimizers × cheap-evaluation tricks), and critical discussion — the two-stage gap (proxy vs final performance, measured by Kendall Tau), weight-sharing bias, random search as a mandatory control, multimodel forgetting in supernets, joint hyperparameter+architecture optimization, resource-aware objectives.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The playbook for the model/config half of self-evolution. The two-stage gap *is* "good validation loss but bad walk-forward Sharpe" — Kendall Tau between promotion proxies and walk-forward outcomes is directly implementable. `sweep_train.py` is naive HPO; hyperband/BOHB successive halving is a drop-in upgrade. Network morphism (function-preserving widen/deepen on plateau) is the most literal "self-evolving" mechanism available — capacity growth without cold start. LwF distillation + iCaRL-style regime-stratified rehearsal are the survey's prescribed anti-forgetting fixes. The random-search-control finding is an evaluation discipline: if a self-modification doesn't beat random perturbation at equal compute, it's decoration.
**Top idea:** Kendall-Tau audit of promotion proxies vs full walk-forward Sharpe **[low]**. Also: hyperband/BOHB sweep upgrade [medium], Net2Wider/Net2Deeper morphism on plateau [medium], rehearsal + LwF distillation in retrains [medium], standing random-search control arm for all self-evolution proposals [low], automated feature-construction search over the FeatureContract [high], resource-aware (latency-budgeted) search objectives [low].
**Key caveat:** CV/NLP-centric, high-SNR objectives; every cheap-evaluation trick has weaker rank correlation with out-of-sample PnL, and search multiplies backtest-overfitting risk. Augmentation-policy search can reward temporal-causality-breaking (leaking) transforms — avoid.

### 2.13 Energy Transformer: Operator Learning for Flow Reconstruction (modern Hopfield)
**Technique:** Reconstruction as pattern completion in a Dense-Associative-Memory-derived architecture: the forward pass is iterative gradient descent on a learned scalar energy (query-key attention + Hopfield term); training snapshots become attractors; inference starts from the observed partial state and descends to the nearest stored minimum. Handles arbitrary observation positions; milliseconds inference; explicitly a *memory* model that fails outside the training distribution.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The strongest architectural blueprint for what the memory bank could become — the modern, trainable version of the deleted `hopfield_retrieval.py`: store episodes as minima of a learned energy, treat the current state as a partial pattern, complete the outcome slots. Two safer transfers first: (1) residual energy / reconstruction error is a principled "have I seen this state before?" familiarity signal for the VETO gate — SETAI currently trades at full confidence in never-seen regimes; (2) masked-patch reconstruction is a ready self-supervised pretraining task for the CNN encoder on abundant unlabeled bars (`self_supervised.py` exists, unwired).
**Top idea:** Masked-reconstruction familiarity gate feeding the risk layer (shrink size / raise the EV bar when the state is far from everything stored) **[medium]**. Also: MAE-style encoder pretraining [medium], differentiable Hopfield attention readout replacing kNN [medium], pattern-completion imputation for feed gaps [low], training-history coverage ablation (25/50/75/100%) to set retention policy [low], cross-sectional energy attention over symbols [high].
**Key caveat:** Fluids are quasi-periodic and high-SNR; the authors explicitly warn completion does not learn dynamics — using it for forward prediction is exactly the extrapolation regime where it degrades to averaged training data (worst during novel regimes). The familiarity gate is far safer than prediction-by-completion. T=12 energy-descent steps multiply inference cost.

### 2.14 Meta-Learning in Neural Networks: A Survey (Hospedales et al., TPAMI)
**Technique:** Formalizes learning-to-learn as bilevel optimization with an explicit meta-objective (validation performance *after* adaptation); taxonomy over meta-representation (initializations, optimizers, learned losses, hyperparameters, curricula, exploration), meta-optimizer (gradient, RL, evolution), and episode design — including single-task online meta-learning, where hyperparameters/losses co-evolve with the base learner on one non-stationary task.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** SETAI is already a meta-learning system without an explicit meta-objective. The memory bank + fusion is a black-box/amortized meta-learner — the class the survey warns generalizes worst out-of-distribution (validating the deterministic risk layer as the non-learned safety net in unseen regimes). Regimes form a natural task distribution for meta-training fast adaptation. Single-task online meta-gradients can replace hand-tuned, silently-staling training constants. The learned-loss literature is the recipe for closing the gap between the training loss (direction CE, `transaction_cost_bps=0`) and the true objective (cost-adjusted, risk-adjusted PnL). Domain-shift episode design turns walk-forward from evaluation into a regime-robustness training signal.
**Top idea:** Domain-shift fold design — build promotion folds where validation sits across a detected regime/vol break, and weight promotion toward those folds **[low]**. Also: online meta-gradient tuning of LR/replay-mix/class-balance [medium], meta-learned instance weighting (Meta-Weight-Net) for noisy labels [medium], Reptile/FOMAML regime-episodic meta-init [high], evolved surrogate trading loss via CMA-ES with backtest Sharpe as fitness [high], PBT-lite population outer loop [medium].
**Key caveat:** Task scarcity and correlation (a handful of autocorrelated regimes, not thousands of i.i.d. episodes) makes meta-overfitting the central risk; validation PnL is very noisy (needs purged/embargoed long windows, deflated metrics); cheap online variants have documented short-horizon bias.

### 2.15 A Survey of Deep Meta-Learning (Huisman et al.)
**Technique:** Unified-notation survey of metric-based (similarity-kernel prediction over a support set), model-based (external memory, MANN/LRUA write rules, fast weights), and optimization-based (MAML family, Reptile, FTML online meta-learning, Bayesian variants) meta-learning, plus the N-way k-shot protocol and its confounds (backbone capacity dominates; simple pretrain+fine-tune beats SOTA meta-learners under task shift; MAML's gains are mostly feature reuse).
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The memory bank is literally the survey's Eq. 7 metric-based learner — but its embedding space is borrowed from the supervised policy objective, never trained so that "similar latent" means "similar forward outcome." Episodic (prototypical) training of the retrieval embedding is the fix. LRUA gives a liftable write/eviction rule preserving rare-regime slots. BMAML-style particle ensembles give a grounded uncertainty signal for the VETO gate. Most importantly, the survey supplies the killer baseline: plain pretrain+fine-tune with the identical encoder — if the memory machinery can't beat it, that decides the architecture question.
**Top idea:** Add the pretrain+fine-tune killer baseline to walk-forward comparisons **[low]**, plus episodic prototypical auxiliary loss so retrieval similarity becomes outcome-predictive **[medium]**. Also: learned relation-head similarity (regime-conditional) [low], LRUA write policy [low], FTML task-buffer continual meta-learning [high], Meta-SGD per-parameter plasticity as principled soft caps on self-modification [medium], Reptile regime adaptation [medium], meta-train/val/test protocol with held-out eras AND symbols [low].
**Key caveat:** Few-shot image benchmarks with stationary p(T) and clean labels; support/query splits within a window can leak forward information unless purged. Regime labels are themselves model outputs — noisy task boundaries corrupt episode construction. Never full second-order MAML at retrain cadence.

### 2.16 JaxMARL-HFT: GPU-Accelerated Multi-Agent RL for HFT
**Technique:** First open GPU MARL environment on market-by-order LOB data; 240x training speedups via episode-parallel vmap rollouts over contiguous tensor storage. Substantive findings: the learned market maker converged to a degenerate near-no-trade policy because rewards weren't normalized by traded volume (never trading is a local optimum); stable training required small discretized menus of pre-parameterized strategies; cross-play matrices exposed weaknesses invisible against a single baseline; promotion metrics were deliberately decoupled from training reward.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Its degeneracy diagnosis is SETAI's diagnosis: `force_directional_flip` treats the symptom the paper explains at the cause (reward/label shaping) — and the flag is enabled only in backtests, breaking backtest/live parity. The strategy-menu action space is the concrete recipe for vision item 2 (known strategies with soft caps): a policy that selects among bounded, pre-parameterized strategy primitives is what trains stably in low-SNR settings. Fixed proxy metrics decoupled from training loss, plus a cross-play pool of frozen past checkpoints and baselines, is a PSRO-lite promotion methodology. Episode-parallel rollouts are the throughput pattern for making frequent re-evaluation affordable.
**Top idea:** Replace force_directional_flip with reward/label reshaping — cost-aware, horizon-scaled flat band; per-trade-normalized outcome weighting; restore parity **[medium]**. Also: strategy-primitive action layer with bounded parameters [medium], decoupled promotion proxy metrics [low], cross-play matrix promotion check [medium], episode-parallel backtest restructuring [high], fill-adversity stress testing [low].
**Key caveat:** HFT/MBO domain; the environment itself is unusable for Alpaca minute bars. "Outperforms baselines" ≠ profitable (the MM still loses 0.2 ticks). Transfer the diagnosis and evaluation patterns, not the system.

### 2.17 Self-Net: Lifelong Learning via Continual Self-Modeling
**Technique:** Decouples learning from storing: each task gets a freely-trained network; a lifelong autoencoder compresses trained weight vectors into tiny latents (10x compression, ~3% relative accuracy loss over 50–100 tasks); reconstructed weights are reloaded on task recurrence. Cosine similarity between original and reconstructed weights ≥ ~0.997 is a validation-data-free performance-preservation proxy; proximal fine-tuning from a common source keeps all task solutions on a compact manifold.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** The real lesson is not the AE (storage isn't SETAI's constraint) but the pattern: *archive regime specialists, retrain freely, reload/distill on recurrence*. The recent "isolate experiment checkpoints" commit is halfway there; checkpoints need regime signatures and a recurrence-triggered use path (safest first use: as distillation teachers during rehearsal, not live swaps). Weight-cosine drift telemetry is a near-zero-cost early warning distinguishing gentle refinement from destructive overwrites. Proximal warm-start (TaskLoss + λ·MSE(θ_canonical, θ)) implements the user's soft caps at the weight level, with λ schedulable (loosen after drawdown, tighten when performing). Latent-manifold sampling over past profitable checkpoints is a long-term generative mechanism for vision item 1.
**Top idea:** Per-module cosine(θ_prev, θ_new) drift telemetry in the retrain path, gating promotion **[low]**. Also: proximal warm-start regularizer [low], regime-tagged specialist archive with reload/distill on recurrence [medium], behavioral rehearsal via archived specialists as teachers [medium], latent-manifold strategy generation (10+ profitable checkpoints first) [high].
**Key caveat:** Assumes discrete labeled tasks trained once; a misfiring regime classifier loads the wrong specialist at the worst time — needs hysteresis/confidence gating. Cosine certifies fidelity to the *original*, not fitness to the *current* market. Plain data replay from the cache may beat weight compression outright.

### 2.18 MarketGANs: Multivariate Financial Time-Series Augmentation
**Technique:** Conditional WGAN-GP + TCN generating joint 98-asset return vectors with a Fama-French factor model as inductive bias: the network emits *bounded multiplicative corrections* around transparent rolling-OLS factor estimates, conditioned on macro covariates. Joint generation captures cross-sectional correlation and tail co-crashes that independent-residual bootstraps destroy. Production recipe: quarterly warm-started retraining, early stopping on a distributional criterion, low-LR fine-tune on the newest slice.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Three structural patterns transfer regardless of the GAN. (1) Structure-as-regularizer: anchoring neural output as a bounded correction around a deterministic baseline is exactly the architecture for fusing a self-evolving brain with known-strategy priors — a proven antidote to the degenerate-policy failures SETAI fought. (2) The rolling retrain protocol (warm-start, few epochs, distributional early stopping, low-LR recent-slice fine-tune) is a cheap continual-adaptation recipe for the retrain loop. (3) The core empirical lesson — per-symbol independence silently destroys tail co-movement, and that error is amplified by the sizing layer — argues the risk manager should evaluate joint cross-symbol scenarios. The perturbed-R² diagnostic (degrade signals to calibrated R² levels, plot Sharpe vs R²) separates signal value from structure value and detects lookahead leakage.
**Top idea:** Perturbed-R² diagnostic in the backtest engine **[low]** and the stylized-facts evaluation module (vol clustering, heavy tails, leverage effect, cross-correlation distance, co-crash probability) **[low]**. Also: multiplicative-correction anchoring for brain outputs [medium], rolling-retrain protocol port [low], regime-conditioned scenario generator — factor bootstrap FIRST, GAN only if justified [high], joint cross-symbol tail stress in the risk manager [medium].
**Key caveat:** Daily large-cap returns, not intraday; the headline Sharpes used controlled look-ahead — with honest forecasts the GAN did not beat simple factor-model covariance. WGAN-GP is finicky and data-hungry; synthetic data can be reward-hacked by a self-evolving learner and must be gated by stylized-facts metrics, never used for final promotion decisions.

### 2.19 NFL / NFL+: No Forgetting Learning (Memory-free Continual Learning)
**Technique:** Uses the previous model as a KD teacher on *new* data (temperature-softened logits) with a stepwise-freezing schedule (heads first, then encoder under KD constraint); NFL+ adds an undercomplete autoencoder penalty keeping task-critical encoder feature directions stable, and a tiny identity-regularized affine logit bias-correction against recency bias. Matches replay methods at ~14.75x less memory; introduces the Plasticity–Stability ratio metric.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Each retrain on recent bars is a "new task" under drift; the NFL recipe is nearly free (the previous promoted checkpoint already exists). Critical SETAI-specific angle: memory-bank keys are encoder embeddings — if a retrain moves the encoder, every stored key silently goes stale (`require_encoder_match` detects but nothing prevents it); NFL+'s AE representation-stability penalty is the targeted regularizer. The recency-bias affine correction addresses directional prior shift after retraining through a one-sided market, complementing Platt calibration. BWT/AF/PS metrics translate directly into promotion gates.
**Top idea:** Self-distillation anchor in the retrain loop: λ·KL(teacher ‖ student) against the frozen promoted checkpoint, exposed in `configs/training.yaml`, λ scheduled down on genuine regime breaks **[low]**. Also: two-phase stepwise-freezing retrain [medium], encoder-drift regularizer / drift-triggered bank re-encoding [medium], backward-transfer promotion gate over 3–6 frozen regime slices [low], PS-ratio telemetry [low], recency-bias logit correction before Platt [low].
**Key caveat:** Class-incremental image setting with clean boundaries; KD assumes the old policy is worth preserving — fixed λ risks institutionalizing stale alpha, so it needs regime/outcome-aware scheduling. Replay wins when memory is allowed (SETAI has it) — KD complements replay, never replaces it. Only the reduced two-phase variant fits a fast retrain cadence.

### 2.20 Room for Improvement (Nature Machine Intelligence editorial, 2021)
**Technique:** A one-page editorial, not a methods paper: AI research lacks biomedical-style ethical oversight; NeurIPS broader-impact statements degenerated into boilerplate without structure; compute cost should be a first-class consideration; accountability in autonomous decision systems is unresolved.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Near-zero technical relevance (likely a mistaken inclusion). The transferable process lesson: systems that act autonomously and modify themselves need *structured, schema-mandated* change documentation, not free-form self-assessment; and accountability-critical logic (risk constraints, session guards) should remain non-learned and auditable — which SETAI's architecture already does by convention but not by tested contract.
**Top idea:** Auto-emit a schema-validated "model change statement" (training window, cost assumptions, walk-forward vs incumbent, known weak regimes) at every checkpoint promotion, stored beside `brain.pt` **[low]**. Also: test-enforced learned-proposes/risk-disposes invariant [low], compute-budget-aware retrain scheduler [medium].
**Key caveat:** No empirical content; pure governance hygiene — improves nothing predictive. Do after promotion wiring, not before. If a technical paper was intended, source the replacement.

### 2.21 SatSOM: Saturation Self-Organizing Maps for Continual Learning
**Technique:** Gives every SOM neuron its own learning rate and neighborhood radius that decay with cumulative update magnitude — saturated (well-trained) neurons freeze, routing new distribution shifts to underutilized regions instead of overwriting old prototypes. Quantile-threshold retrieval (only the nearest ~0.1–1% of saturated neurons vote) is essential on imbalanced data. A Growing variant adapts capacity online; the saturation map itself is a novelty/consolidation visualization.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** A principled outcome-driven plasticity schedule for the memory bank: per-slot learning rates decaying with exposure means rare-but-critical regime prototypes (crashes, squeezes) stabilize and can't be dragged toward the current regime — directly attacking the brain/memory degeneracy failure mode. Quantile-threshold retrieval fixes imbalanced coverage (crisis prototypes outvoted by thousands of chop-day neighbors). BMU saturation is a free familiarity signal (unsaturated best-match = never-consolidated state → size down / VETO). The saturation idea generalizes: per-unit cumulative-update damping in the brain is a cheap replay-free EWC alternative.
**Top idea:** Quantile-threshold retrieval in `query_top_k_simple` (normalize distances, q-quantile cutoff, weight by (1−d)^p) **[low]**. Also: per-slot maturity/saturation field on MemoryItem [medium], SatSOM regime map over encoder embeddings with saturation-as-novelty [medium], saturation-based per-unit LR damping in retrains [medium], growth trigger for dynamic memory capacity [medium], low-saturation BMU-hit fraction as a drift alarm [low].
**Key caveat:** Image benchmarks with disjoint classes; markets exhibit true concept drift where old mappings become actively *wrong*, not just dormant — saturation-frozen knowledge needs a staleness/pruning policy on top. Trails alternatives on diverse embedding spaces (SETAI's is exactly that). Many hyperparameters; label-prototype updates need rework for continuous heavy-tailed outcomes.

### 2.22 Automatic Machine Learning Method for Hyper-parameter Search (HPO mini-survey)
**Technique:** Short survey + MNIST benchmark of HPO strategies: grid, random, simulated annealing, naive evolution, Hyperband successive halving, PBT, BOHB, TPE. TPE found a better config ~4x faster than random search; multi-fidelity methods get most of the win by killing bad configs early; different strategies converge to genuinely different optima (hyperparameters interact).
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Maps onto exactly one component: `scripts/sweep_train.py` and the retrain loop's configuration. SETAI evolves weights but its hyperparameters (LR, encoder widths, class-balance weights, fusion thresholds, target horizon) are frozen hand-set YAML constants — this paper argues those should be searched per data regime, extending vision item 3 to the training configuration itself. TPE/Optuna is a drop-in; Hyperband budgets map onto fast_mode and walk-forward folds; PBT is the philosophically closest method to a self-evolving bot (population of checkpoints whose hyperparameters exploit/explore between retrain cycles, with the promotion gate choosing the canonical member).
**Top idea:** Replace the sweep grid with Optuna TPE over a persisted study, objective = mean walk-forward Sharpe (deflated) **[low]**. Also: Hyperband/ASHA pruning using fast_mode as low fidelity [medium], PBT loop of 4–8 checkpoints [high], search fusion/gating thresholds within hard risk guardrails [medium], multi-fold noise-aware scalarized objective (Sharpe − λ·maxDD − μ·turnover) [medium].
**Key caveat:** Weak introductory paper (single MNIST experiment, 0.1% deltas). Naive TPE/BOHB on a single backtest window is a data-snooping machine — purged/embargoed multi-fold evaluation and deflated metrics are mandatory; aggressive early pruning can discard regime-robust configs; PBT must be sandboxed behind the promotion gate.

### 2.23 Adaptive-Wave Alternative for the Black-Scholes Model (econophysics)
**Technique:** Replaces the Black-Scholes PDE with a nonlinear Schrödinger equation whose "market-heat potential" is a learnable kernel mixture updated by a continuous Hebbian rule (leaky decay + co-activation reinforcement); extends to a coupled price+volatility Manakov system interpreted as a bidirectional associative memory (Kosko BAM). No real-market validation anywhere; the only fit is circular (to Black-Scholes itself) with degenerate recovered parameters.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Only three structural patterns transfer, not the physics. (1) The leaky-Hebbian update w ← (1−λ)w + c·retrieval_strength·outcome_signal is a clean form for memory-entry weight updates: decay-unless-reinforced gives bounded weights, automatic staleness handling, and drift adaptation. (2) BAM-style paired (state, outcome) storage supports reverse queries — "retrieve state signatures that preceded large adverse excursions" as a data-driven danger-proximity input to the VETO gate. (3) Treating volatility as a co-evolving state variable motivates an auxiliary volatility-forecast head and leverage-effect regime features; kernel-mixture soft caps over a "market temperature" scalar are a template for self-tuning limits.
**Top idea:** Leaky-Hebbian update rule for memory-entry weights in the updater **[low]**. Also: BAM-style reverse (danger-set) retrieval feeding the VETO gate [medium], market-temperature-modulated soft caps [medium], auxiliary vol-forecast head [medium], Levenberg-Marquardt for small parametric fits (Platt curves, sizing curves) [low], parametric shock-overlay stress scenarios (exp-OU vol + tanh shock) for risk testing only [medium].
**Key caveat:** Speculative, unvalidated, non-identifiable; the quantum framing is metaphorical and the Elliott-wave motivation is folklore. Never implement the NLS/soliton apparatus as a predictive model. Even the transferable patterns need own validation (Hebbian decay rate interacts with trade frequency).

### 2.24 Auto-PyTorch: Multi-Fidelity Meta-Learning for Efficient and Robust AutoDL
**Technique:** Joint architecture+hyperparameter optimization via BOHB (TPE-style BO + Hyperband, epochs as fidelity), warmstarted by a meta-learned greedy portfolio of ~16 configs that transfers across datasets (<0.1% regret vs evaluating 2,000 configs), finished with Caruana-style greedy weighted ensembling of top-k DNN checkpoints *plus traditional baselines* (LightGBM/CatBoost/RF). Fidelity rank correlations must be checked, not assumed; hyperparameter importance is concentrated and stable across budgets.
**Why it matters for SETAI:** Formalizes the substrate SETAI already has (retrain loop, promotion, walk-forward, sweep script). Three specific mappings: (1) multi-fidelity search wrapped around retrains fits a nightly compute window; (2) the greedy portfolio is an associative memory over *configurations* — persist (config × fold/regime) performance and warmstart every future sweep with configs proven across past regimes (the missing feedback path from training outcomes into how the brain rebuilds itself); (3) Caruana ensemble selection over brain checkpoints + classical baselines (LightGBM on FeatureContract features, momentum/mean-revert rules) is a principled mechanism for vision item 2 — the greedily learned validation weights ARE the soft caps, re-learned from outcomes each retrain. Multi-seed t-tests upgrade promotion from point estimates to statistics.
**Top idea:** Fidelity-correlation audit (Spearman between short-budget and full-budget results per regime) before enabling any aggressive early stopping **[low]**; then Caruana post-hoc ensemble of checkpoints + non-neural baselines **[high impact, high difficulty]**. Also: multi-fidelity sweep in sweep_train.py [medium], config portfolio memory [medium], fANOVA importance analysis to shrink the search space [low], shaped (funnel) parameterization + multi-seed promotion t-tests [low].
**Key caveat:** i.i.d. tabular classification; in markets a short training window is a *different regime*, not a cheap proxy — the correlation audit is mandatory. Portfolio entries need decay/refresh under regime shift; a 30-member ensemble raises intraday inference cost against the recent symbol-batching wins.

### 3.2 Lens: Trading Strategy Evaluation

**Overall finding:** SETAI has the skeleton of a serious evaluation stack — bar-driven BacktestEngine with deterministic risk gating, ~22 metrics in `performance.py`, WalkForwardRunner with embargo support, per-run provenance JSON — but it cannot currently answer the one question a self-evolving trader must answer: *is the new strategy actually better, net of costs, out of sample, versus honest benchmarks?* Four structural failures dominate. (1) **Costs and fills are optimistic and inconsistent**: decisions and fills both use the same bar's close with constant 2 bps slippage/1 bps fee (`engine.py`, `fills.py`); historical bars carry `spread=0` while live bars carry real spreads; training labels use `transaction_cost_bps=0` — every evaluated edge is inflated exactly where intraday edges live. (2) **Walk-forward never retrains per fold**; every fold scores the same global checkpoint that may have seen the test window — the "out-of-sample" numbers are not out-of-sample; reporting ranks by raw mean Sharpe with no deflated-Sharpe correction or paired statistics. (3) **Benchmark discipline is broken**: baselines exist only in backtest, are effectively untradeable through the EV gate, and the `--compare` harness prints hardcoded zero fusion counts. (4) **Credit assignment is corrupted** per the shield-as-environment principle: memory items record `action_annotation=None`, no clamped/vetoed flag exists, and `force_directional_flip` breaks backtest/live parity. Synthetic/GAN data, stylized-facts checks, cross-sectional signals, and CVaR evaluation are entirely absent. The good news: nearly all P0 fixes are evaluation-layer changes a solo developer can land without touching the model.

## 4. Unified Research-Adoption Roadmap

The two lens roadmaps interleave naturally: the evaluation lens's honesty fixes are preconditions for the memory lens's learning-loop work (a self-evolving loop trained and promoted on dishonest numbers evolves toward artifacts). Phases are ordered by impact/risk; within a phase, items are roughly independent and can be parallelized.

### Phase 1 — Honest measurement + close the loop (P0; ~all low/medium difficulty; do first)

*Nothing downstream is trustworthy until this phase lands. Every item is an evaluation-layer or training-loop change; none touches model architecture.*

1. **Cost and fill honesty** — `src/backtest/engine.py`, `src/backtest/fills.py`, `src/backtest/performance.py`, `configs/*.yaml`: add `fill_timing: same_close|next_open` (next_open default for promotion), slippage = base_bps + k·spread_bps, `cvar_95` metric, realistic `training.transaction_cost_bps` shared between labels and evaluation. *(Cross-Market Execution)*
2. **Data integrity** — build `src/evaluation/stylized_facts.py` and run it over the historical cache immediately; fix the split-unadjusted cache (NVDA 10:1 discontinuities) with adjusted bars before further training. *(MarketGANs)* — pulled forward from P1 because contaminated data poisons everything else in this phase.
3. **Benchmark integrity** — `src/strategies/policy_factory.py`, `scripts/run_backtest.py`: make momentum/mean-reversion/buy-and-hold tradeable through the EV gate; repair or delete `--compare`; require candidate > buy-and-hold and > incumbent on identical bars. *(JaxMARL-HFT, AutoML survey)*
4. **Credit assignment** — `src/memory/schemas.py`, `src/strategies/memory_policy.py`, `tick_processor`, `BacktestEngine.run`: record executed (post-risk) action, clamp/veto flag, rejection reason on every decision; `intervention_rate` in runtime_status. *(Cross-Market Execution shield principle)*
5. **Degeneracy fixed at the cause** — `src/training/targets.py`, `src/training/losses.py`: cost-widened, √horizon-scaled flat band; per-trade-normalized loss weighting; unify or delete `force_directional_flip` so backtest and runtime construct identical policies. *(JaxMARL-HFT)*
6. **Genuine out-of-sample walk-forward + authoritative promotion** — `src/evaluation/walk_forward.py`, `src/training/checkpoint_promotion.py`, `scripts/run_train.py`: per-fold retraining (fast_mode) or hard chronology assertions; paired candidate-vs-incumbent folds with paired t-test/sign test; drop degenerate folds; PromotionGate extended with trading metrics (WF Sharpe, maxDD, CVaR95, benchmark-relative) and wired so `brain.pt` can never be overwritten ungated; runtime bootstrap reads `checkpoints/registry.json`. *(AutoML two-stage gap, Cross-Market paired stats)*
7. **Wire the retrain loop skeleton** — `src/training/retrain.py`: `check_retrain_trigger` called on a schedule; retrains flow through the promotion gate. *(Prerequisite for all self-evolution.)*
8. **Forgetting-safe retraining** — `src/training/trainer.py`, `configs/training.yaml`: λ·KL self-distillation against the frozen promoted checkpoint + L2 proximal anchor (λ schedulable, loosened on detected regime breaks); regime-stratified rehearsal quotas from the historical cache in every retrain batch. *(NFL/NFL+, DEN, TACO, Self-Net, AutoML LwF/iCaRL)*
9. **Measure forgetting** — `src/evaluation/walk_forward.py`, PromotionGate: 3–6 frozen held-out slices from distinct historical regime windows; AP/AF matrix; backward-transfer floor blocks promotion of checkpoints that unlearn old regimes. *(TACO, NFL)*
10. **Make memory matter (stage 1)** — `src/strategies/memory_policy.py`: blend retrieved neighbor outcome statistics into TradeProposal `expected_gain/loss_bps` as a bounded prior (e.g., max ±30%) ahead of the RiskManager EV gate; fix `action_annotation=None`. *(D2Skill stage 1)*
11. **Weight-space telemetry** — retrain path + `runtime_status.py`: per-module cosine(θ_prev, θ_new) and norm trajectories; alerts on monotonic shrink or frozen checkpoints; contrast-gap/embedding-similarity monitor on training pairs. *(Self-Net, DEN, Self-Replication, Temporal Self-Rewarding)*

### Phase 2 — Causal memory + measured risk (P1)

1. **Memory hygiene** — `src/memory/updater.py`, `memory_bank.py`: novelty-gated writes (merge within ε instead of duplicate insert), temporal-adjacency/same-symbol exclusion, enforce the unused `min_similarity` floor, salience write-gate + uncertainty read-gate, protected set for tail episodes, priority eviction replacing FIFO, quantile-threshold retrieval; delete the ~80–120 dead MemoryConfig fields and inert GUI toggles. *(AiGAS, MemMamba, TACO, SatSOM)*
2. **Counterfactual memory credit (stage 2)** — `src/evaluation/walk_forward.py`, `src/memory/schemas.py`: paired brain-only vs memory-fused runs as the memory-bank promotion gate; per-item utility EMA credited only when fusion changed the action; retrieval ranked by α·sim + (1−α)(utility + UCB); utility-based pruning with protection window; retire internalized memories whose counterfactual utility decays post-retrain. *(D2Skill)*
3. **Encoder/memory compatibility guard** — promotion path: re-encode the bank with the new encoder on promotion, or add an encoder-representation stability penalty during retraining; encoder fingerprint as a hard staleness alarm. *(NFL+)*
4. **Perturbed-R² diagnostic + cap sweeps** — `scripts/run_backtest.py`, `run_cap_sweep.py`: signal-degradation harness (Sharpe vs calibrated R²); risk-cap sweeps with mean/var/CVaR knee-finding. *(MarketGANs, Cross-Market Execution)*
5. **Strategy arbitration layer ("known strategies with soft caps")** — new arbiter in `src/strategies/`, `runtime_bootstrap.py`, `risk_manager.py`, execution schemas: registry of brain + bounded strategy primitives (momentum, mean-revert, passive/aggressive exit, flat), `strategy_id` threaded through proposals/orders/fills, per-strategy notional budgets in RiskConfig, outcome-weighted allocation (PnL-EMA to start). *(JaxMARL-HFT, D2Skill regime tier, Auto-PyTorch ensembling as the eventual weighting mechanism)*
6. **Domain-shift fold design** — walk-forward fold construction that deliberately places validation across detected regime/vol breaks, weighted into promotion. *(Hospedales)*
7. **Guardrail-toggle diagnostic** — backtest log-only risk mode; would-be-violation curve tracked across retrain generations as a degeneracy early warning. *(Cross-Market Execution)*
8. **Anchored negatives + pair-validity filter** — retrain dataset construction: frozen-brain/baseline worst decisions retained permanently as negatives (regime-conditioned); contrasts formed only when outcome gap > round-trip cost + noise buffer. *(Temporal Self-Rewarding)*
9. **Adaptive curriculum sampler** — replay-buffer wrapper scheduling regime buckets easy→hard by measured per-bucket accuracy; verifier-hardening audit of the reward path (enumerate and reject degenerate policies in promotion). *(LADDER)*

### Phase 3 — Regime awareness, richer features, smarter search (P2)

1. **Familiarity/OOD gate** — small masked-reconstruction readout on the feature window; normalized residual feeds position sizing and the EV bar. Also MAE-style self-supervised encoder pretraining via the existing `self_supervised.py`. *(Energy Transformer)*
2. **Regime-snapshot recurrence layer** — per-session K-means centroids over regime features, two-stage MMD/Mahalanobis matching; on match activate per-regime Platt params/retrieval weights/sizing caps; on no-match emit a novelty event tightening risk caps; prequential error over resolved trades as the live rollback/retrain trigger. *(AiGAS)*
3. **Cross-sectional market context** — `src/features/market_context.py`: SPY/QQQ returns and vol, relative strength, universe breadth, wired into the FeatureContract with a version bump. *(MarketGANs cross-sectional lesson)*
4. **Episodic embedding training + killer baseline** — prototypical auxiliary loss so retrieval similarity predicts outcomes; standing walk-forward comparison against plain pretrain+fine-tune with the identical encoder. *(Huisman)*
5. **Sweep upgrade** — Optuna TPE + Hyperband/ASHA pruning in `sweep_train.py` with walk-forward Sharpe objective, fidelity-correlation (Spearman) audit before enabling aggressive pruning, Kendall-Tau audit of promotion proxies, fANOVA importance to shrink the search space, config-portfolio memory warmstarting future sweeps, multi-seed promotion t-tests. *(HPO survey, He survey, Auto-PyTorch)*
6. **Synthetic scenarios (bootstrap only)** — regime-conditioned block-bootstrap + rolling-factor-beta generator in `src/training/augmentation.py` for rare-regime oversampling; correlated multi-symbol tail stress in the risk manager; shock-overlay (exp-OU vol + tanh shock) stress of session guards; adverse-fill mode in the backtest simulator with baseline-vs-adverse reporting in promotion. All gated by stylized-facts; never used for final promotion decisions. *(MarketGANs, Adaptive-Wave, JaxMARL-HFT)*
7. **Memory plasticity schedules** — per-slot maturity/saturation fields (decay-unless-reinforced, leaky-Hebbian form w ← (1−λ)w + c·retrieval·outcome); LRUA-style write/eviction; prototype splitting on regime-bimodal outcome stats; BAM-style reverse "danger set" retrieval feeding the VETO gate. *(SatSOM, Huisman, Adaptive-Wave, DEN)*
8. **Regime-tagged specialist archive** — regime signatures in checkpoint meta; archived specialists used as distillation teachers during rehearsal (not live swaps); noise-robustness promotion gate (perturb weights, require stable behavior); recency-bias affine logit correction before Platt. *(Self-Net, Self-Replication, NFL+)*
9. **Governance** — schema-validated model-change statement at promotion; test-enforced learned-proposes/risk-disposes contract; self-evolution ladder in configs (per-axis autonomy policy); automated per-retrain error-analysis artifact in the GUI. *(Nature editorial, Co-Improvement)*

### Phase 4 — Experiment-gated advanced mechanisms (P3; each must beat a plain fine-tune baseline AND an equal-budget random-search control)

1. **PBT-lite population outer loop** — 3–5 concurrent checkpoints with perturbed hyperparameters, exploit/explore between retrain cycles, promotion gate picks the canonical member; optionally cross-distillation between members (fitness-augmented soup). *(HPO survey, Hospedales, Self-Replication)*
2. **Small SOM regime map** — 8×8 toroidal PSOM auxiliary head behind experiment isolation; per-cell outcome stats as memory index; assignment entropy → sizing; Moran's-index acceptance test; GUI regime-map tab. *(DPSOM, SatSOM)*
3. **Differentiable Hopfield/attention retrieval** — energy-attention readout over the bank (learned temperature and projections) trained end-to-end on outcomes — only after counterfactual utility (Phase 2) proves memory adds value. *(Energy Transformer, MemMamba)*
4. **Meta-level learning** — online meta-gradient tuning of LR/replay-mix/class-balance; meta-learned instance weighting; Reptile/FOMAML regime-episodic meta-init for nightly retrains; evolved surrogate trading loss (CMA-ES, backtest Sharpe fitness) — all require purged/embargoed splits and deflated metrics first. *(Hospedales, Huisman)*
5. **Capacity growth** — Net2Wider/Net2Deeper morphism or DEN-style threshold-triggered head expansion with group lasso on sustained plateau; DEN-Finetune as an offline capacity estimator. *(He survey, DEN)*
6. **Generative strategy discovery** — future-guided positive selection via experiment checkpoints (adopt a candidate's decisions as targets only where they out-score the incumbent); reflection-on-failure trade autopsies writing corrective memories; latent-manifold sampling over 10+ profitable checkpoints; TTRL-style bounded episodic head fine-tune before low-confidence decisions (frozen encoder, discard after). *(Temporal Self-Rewarding, D2Skill, Self-Net, LADDER)*
7. **Caruana mixed ensemble** — greedy weighted ensemble over top-k checkpoints + non-neural baselines (LightGBM/CatBoost on FeatureContract features, rule strategies) as the mature soft-cap weighting mechanism, budgeted against intraday inference latency. *(Auto-PyTorch)*
8. **Cross-play promotion pool + episode-parallel backtesting** — candidate vs frozen pool of past promoted checkpoints and baselines across regime slices; tensor-contiguous episode-parallel rollout restructuring of the backtest engine for cheap frequent re-evaluation. *(JaxMARL-HFT)*

## 5. Explicit "Do NOT Do" List

Paper ideas that are poor fits for SETAI, with reasons drawn from the papers' own caveats:

1. **Do not implement the NLS/soliton/quantum pricing model** (Adaptive-Wave). Empirically unvalidated, circularly fitted, non-identifiable parameters (negative volatility scalings); the quantum framing is metaphorical and the Elliott-wave motivation is folklore. Only the leaky-Hebbian, BAM, and soft-cap-kernel *patterns* transfer.
2. **Do not build a WGAN-GP market generator now** (MarketGANs). With honest forecasts the GAN failed to beat simple factor-model covariance; WGAN-GP is unstable and data-hungry (25 years × 98 assets); SETAI lacks the scale, and a self-evolving learner can reward-hack generator artifacts. Factor/block bootstrap first, gated by stylized-facts, never for final promotion decisions.
3. **Do not do per-bar TTRL-style micro-training intraday** (LADDER). ~100 RL steps per hard instance is infeasible at bar cadence; restrict to small heads, rare low-confidence triggers, or overnight per-regime adapters — and only after the broad base model exists (TTRL does nothing on a weak base).
4. **Do not restore the deleted ESOM/Hopfield/dendritic stack wholesale.** The surveys (Hospedales, Huisman) show black-box memory learners are weakest out-of-distribution and plain fine-tune baselines often beat elaborate machinery; DPSOM's own enrichment NMIs are tiny. Rebuild leaner, in the Phase order above, behind experiment gates — and meanwhile delete the dead ESOM/topology config fields and GUI toggles that misrepresent capabilities.
5. **Do not use Energy-Transformer pattern completion for forward prediction** (Energy Transformer). The authors explicitly warn it completes snapshots and does not learn dynamics; in novel regimes it regresses to averaged training data — exactly when losses are worst. The familiarity gate is the safe half.
6. **Do not adopt alpha-shape IoU regime matching** (AiGAS). Computationally infeasible and geometrically meaningless beyond ~5–6 dimensions; substitute MMD/Mahalanobis/embedding cosine. Also never hard-swap models on a regime match — use retrieved regime knowledge as a prior/gate.
7. **Do not run full NAS or entire-structure architecture search** (He survey). Even "cheap" NAS assumes GPU-days amortized over a long-lived static task; market drift stales searched architectures within months. Only the continual/cheap techniques transfer (hyperband, morphism, rehearsal, random-search controls).
8. **Do not use full second-order MAML or the full five-step NFL pipeline** (Huisman, NFL). Second-order gradients are too expensive at retrain cadence (Reptile/FOMAML only); NFL's five sequential phases conflict with fast_mode — use the two-phase variant.
9. **Do not live-swap regime-specialist checkpoints on raw regime-detector signals** (Self-Net). A misfiring classifier loads the wrong specialist at the worst time; require hysteresis/confidence gating, and prefer specialists as distillation teachers first.
10. **Do not build the zero-knowledge audit layer** (Cross-Market Execution). It was mocked even in the paper and is irrelevant to a single-user paper-trading bot; only the plain audit-trail idea transfers.
11. **Do not treat the DPO vanishing-gradient theorem or LLM self-rewarding machinery literally** (Temporal Self-Rewarding). Only the qualitative contrast-collapse failure mode transfers; validate empirically before building on it. Do not anchor negatives to a frozen M0 without regime conditioning — stale-regime "bad" decisions may be regime-inappropriate rather than genuinely bad.
12. **Do not run unconstrained HPO/meta-optimization against a single backtest window** (HPO survey, Hospedales, Auto-PyTorch). It is a data-snooping machine. Purged/embargoed multi-fold evaluation, deflated metrics, multi-seed tests, and fidelity-correlation audits are mandatory preconditions; aggressive early pruning can systematically discard regime-robust configs.
13. **Do not add Self-Net's autoencoder weight compression.** Storage is not SETAI's constraint; .pt files are cheap at this model size and plain data replay from the historical cache likely beats weight compression.
14. **Do not adopt MemMamba's SSM architecture for its long-context capability.** SETAI's intraday lookbacks (hundreds of bars) sit where CNN windows are adequate; take the gating/eviction/probe patterns only. Revisit only if multi-day minute-bar lookbacks (5–10k steps) are ever needed.
15. **Do not use temporal augmentation that breaks causality** (He survey). Mixup/time-warping can leak future information, and augmentation-policy search can reward exactly the leaking transforms.
16. **Do not keep `force_directional_flip` as the answer to the no-trade attractor** (JaxMARL-HFT). It treats the symptom, breaks backtest/live parity, and the paper shows the analogous forced-quote variant *reduced* performance; fix the reward/label shaping instead.
17. **Do not let self-generated tasks/rewards/objectives run autonomously** (Co-Improvement). Keep objective changes, architecture growth, and strategy-code changes behind the human promotion gate; in low-SNR markets, self-generated rewards amplify noise. Every self-modification must pass the fixed versioned regime-stratified benchmark and beat the random-search control.
18. **Do not expect technical content from the Nature MI editorial** — governance hygiene only; if a technical paper was intended for that slot, source the replacement.
19. **Do not treat a weight-space fixpoint as a goal** (Self-Replication). Markets want controlled plasticity; the fixpoint machinery is for diagnostics and stabilizers, never an objective.
20. **Do not trust per-cell/per-regime expectancy as alpha** (DPSOM, SatSOM). Enrichment signals were weak even in favorable domains; use them strictly as priors and gates, with the deterministic risk manager as the non-learned safety layer in unseen regimes.

## 6. Cross-Cutting Principles (distilled from all 24 papers)

1. **Verifier first, learner second** (LADDER, Co-Improvement): the backtest/walk-forward stack is SETAI's verifier — harden it against gaming before amplifying the learner.
2. **Learn from executed actions, not intent** (Cross-Market Execution): every outcome-driven update keys off the post-risk-manager action.
3. **Counterfactual, not correlational credit** (D2Skill): a memory/strategy earns utility only for decisions it actually changed.
4. **Anchor every self-update** (NFL, DEN, Self-Net, Self-Replication, LADDER): KD/proximal/KL anchors to the promoted checkpoint are the universal cheap insurance against collapse and forgetting.
5. **Protect the rare** (TACO, SatSOM, Huisman LRUA): under any capacity bound or rebalancing, crisis-regime episodes vanish first unless explicitly protected — and they are the memories the risk path needs most.
6. **Promotion is a statistical decision** (Cross-Market, Auto-PyTorch, He survey): paired folds, significance tests, backward-transfer floors, benchmark-relative requirements, proxy-audits.
7. **Every clever mechanism must beat two dumb baselines** (Huisman, He survey): plain pretrain+fine-tune, and equal-budget random search. If it can't, it's decoration.
